## Typography

- **Unbounded** (SemiBold 600): headings and the logo.
- **Golos Text**: body text.
- **JetBrains Mono**: labels and numbers.

All three are open-source fonts licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/). They are not covered by the licence of this repository.

## How to write the name

- **Medics Help**: two words, capital M and capital H.
- Not "MedicsHelp", "Medics-Help", "Medic Help" or "MEDICS HELP".
- The domain is written in lower case: **medics.help**.
- In the logo, "Medics" is in the ink colour and "Help" in teal.

## Please do not change the logo

Use the files as they are. Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the logo, do not change the font, and keep clear space around it. If you need a version that is not here, write to [info@medics.help](mailto:info@medics.help).
